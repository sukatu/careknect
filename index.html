OUR BELIEF
“No one should lose their way between needing care and receiving it.”

Careknect brings together health-sector understanding, thoughtful collaboration and purposeful technology to support more coordinated experiences.

WHY WE EXIST

To help care remain
connected around people.

Healthcare is delivered through many people, places and moments. When those parts do not connect, the experience can become difficult to navigate.

Careknect exists to strengthen the relationships and systems that hold the journey together—from first contact through continued care.
